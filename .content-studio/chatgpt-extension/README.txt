LOCAL CONTENT STUDIO — CHATGPT COMPANION

One-time installation:
1. Click “Open ChatGPT” in the local studio, or load this folder as an unpacked
   Chrome extension and open https://chatgpt.com/.
2. Sign into ChatGPT in that window once.
3. Keep the tab open while a post is being written.

The companion talks only to http://127.0.0.1:3002. It pastes the studio prompt
into ordinary ChatGPT chat and sends the finished answer back. It does not use
Codex and does not read passwords or cookies.
